Press kit

Description

Nightstar Gambit is an anime space opera roguelite. Take one ship through endless enemies, build a new arsenal every run, and fight alongside a crew of anime protagonists who each star in their own comedy season.

You command the Nightstar, a dreadnaught built around one gravity array beam cannon and parked at the back of the war. You're the captain, and you're finally going to fire it. Pilots launch in fighters and mecha, the drone crew runs the swarm, and the whole bridge talks through every fight.

Every crew member stars in a season of their own, told as voiced anime episodes that play out across your runs. The fights bring you back for one more run, and the crew brings you back for one more episode.

Why this game

Nightstar Gambit started as a "wouldn't it be cool". By day I'm a lead gameplay engineer, and I grew up on decades of anime and video games: Space Battleship Yamato, Macross, Robotech, Gal Force and every space opera since. What if you could fly one of those giant beam cannon ships, with a silly anime crew, in a roguelite? It was the idea I finally wanted to see all the way through, so this year I started building it.

From there it was a series of questions. Which anime archetypes belong on this ship, and how do they become a crew that's fun to fly with? How does the beam cannon work? Can I have a cool fighter, and a mecha? What about drones? What would it take to get writers to tell these characters' stories, an artist to draw them, voice actors to play them, and badass synthwave to fly to? Every answer led to the next question, and the answers became the game.

Features

  • One enormous gun. Your weapons fire on their own. The beam cannon is yours: charge it, aim it and pick the moment it ends a fight.
  • A crew that is the story. Every crew member is the main character. Each stars in a season of voiced episodes that plays out across your runs.
  • Chart a course to the enemy homeworld. Pick your route system by system, outrun the hunter fleets on your tail and break the gate keepers guarding each sector's jump gate. The crew's episodes play out between the fights.
  • Level up mid-battle. Every level-up deals new cards that change how your weapons fire. Fill up to ten hardpoints, launch fighters, mecha and drones flown by your crew, and unlock new gear for the next run.
  • Bosses with titles longer than their health bars. Every boss arrives with an anime cut-in and a name that grows each phase, until you are fighting Ultra Dai Albedo of the Apotheosis Abyss Absolute.
