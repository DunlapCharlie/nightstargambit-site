Press kit

Description

A retro anime space opera roguelite game. Command the Nightstar, a dreadnaught built around one enormous beam cannon, and a crew of prima-donna anime spoofs who each star in their own fully voiced episodes.

Nightstar Gambit is a 3D arcade space-combat roguelite styled after retro anime. You command the Nightstar, a dreadnaught built around one ridiculous spinal beam cannon, and every run is a dash across the star chart to blow up the enemy homeworld.

What makes it stand out is the crew. Most roguelites have one campaign plot. Nightstar Gambit gives every crew member a season of their own. Each one is a prima donna and an open spoof of a famous anime archetype, and each stars in a fully voiced season told as anime episodes that play out across your runs.

The goal is a roguelite that plays like binge-watching a comedy anime: the fights bring you back for one more run, and the crew brings you back for one more episode.

Features

  • One enormous gun. Your weapons fire on their own. The beam cannon is yours: charge it, aim it and pick the moment it ends a fight.
  • A crew that is the story. The Nightstar is crewed by prima-donna anime archetypes. Each one is a menace in their own way, and each stars in a fully voiced season of episodes that plays out across your runs.
  • Chart a course to the enemy homeworld. Pick your route system by system, outrun the hunter fleets on your tail and smash through the gate keepers in your way. The crew's episodes play out between the fights.
  • Level up mid-battle. Every level-up deals new cards that change how your weapons fire. Fill up to ten hardpoints, launch fighters, mecha and drones flown by your crew, and unlock new gear for the next run.
  • Bosses with titles longer than their health bars. Every boss arrives with an anime cut-in and a name that grows each phase, until you are fighting Ultra Dai Albedo of the Apotheosis Abyss Absolute. Its attacks are named too.
  • Comedy first. The crew are menaces and the bosses take themselves very seriously. The game's own full title is The Odyssey of the Night Star Dreadnaught-class Gravity Array Beam Cannon.
